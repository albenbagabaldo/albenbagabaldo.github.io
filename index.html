    Pedestrian safety research presented at ASCE ICTD

    At the 2025 ASCE International Conference on Transportation & Development, I presented work using probabilistic models and Monte Carlo simulation to examine pedestrian safety at intersections. The study considers signal phase, vehicle proximity, and pedestrian waiting time, and explores potential design strategies for safer crossings.
